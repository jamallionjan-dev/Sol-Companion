## How we work, learned today (keep)

- A shape is judged from renders made OUTSIDE the game (`05_TOOLS/sol_cloth/out`, not in git) before anything is
  installed. Show Jamal the picture first; install only what he accepted.
- Do not switch on ready-made modes of the body patch to "see what happens" on his session; read, render, then decide.
- When a look problem is known to artists, search how they solve it before inventing a method.
- `BraGeometry` cannot be run outside the game (it looks its cap surface up by exact canonical coordinates);
  `SolUnderCloth.compute` can, and is tested that way.
- The body patch's on-disk motion cache is keyed on the bytes of its own classes: a change of the shape DATA alone does
  not invalidate it. Change the version string in `PatchUnderCloth.java` when the shape changes.
