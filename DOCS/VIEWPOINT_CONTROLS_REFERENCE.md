## Notes shown by Viewpoint

- These are Viewpoint's own bindings; the game's controls remain unchanged.
- To rebind: click a key and press the new one. Shift, Ctrl, or Alt may be held with it. `Esc` cancels the rebind.
- A mouse button may be bound except left and right click.
- The settings window and the game's Mod Options also expose these bindings.
- Mouse wheel chooses in the loot menu while it is shown. Right click opens the game's context menu at the cursor when the cursor is free.
